🏢 About MrKolapie Pty Ltd

MrKolapie Pty Ltd is a South African registered digital infrastructure and design studio headquartered in Lenasia, Gauteng. The company operates as a B-BBEE Level 1 Contributor, qualifying procurement partners for 135% recognition under the B-BBEE Act.

🚀 Platform Architecture

The MrKolapie ecosystem consists of four integrated ventures:

  • JoziResume.com — Career optimization and Gauteng employment marketplace (LIVE)
  • Jozi Bounce Gaming — Proprietary physics-based game engine and asset suite (Beta)
  • Digital Asset Hub — Vector, brand, and design production environment
  • Steel Infrastructure Division — Physical structural fabrication for SA industrial sites
